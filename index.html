Before placing an order

Build a shortlist you can check

Save the details you would need to identify the same item a week later. Titles and thumbnail photos can look identical across different sellers.

How to verify OssBuy product links

Save the original Taobao, Weidian or 1688 product link alongside the seller name and listing ID. When you paste the URL into the agent’s search, compare the product title, variant photos and seller size chart. If a saved link opens a different item, verify the replacement against the seller and listing ID before using it.

For sneaker size conversion, start with foot length in centimeters and check the model’s size chart. For a jacket, lay a similar garment flat and compare chest width, shoulder width, sleeve length and back length. Record whether the seller gives a flat width or a full circumference.

The photo desk

Which detail should you ask to see?

Brand photographs are visual references. Ask for photos of your own item to compare construction, dimensions and visible condition.

After the item arrives at the warehouse

Review the parcel before dispatch

How to read OssBuy QC photos

Compare warehouse photos with your saved variant notes. For sneakers, look for a matching pair and a readable size label. For a jersey, check the print placement and number. For a set, confirm both pieces are present. If a measurement photo cuts off one end of the tape, request a full view while you can still act on the seller’s return policy.

OssBuy shipping estimate and landed cost

Budget for the item price, domestic postage, applicable agent fees, currency conversion and international delivery. Ask for packed weight and dimensions before parcel consolidation. A bulky jacket or shoe box can increase volumetric weight, so compare what the route actually charges for each packing option.

When comparing quotes for the US, UK or an EU destination, use the delivery postal code where requested. Check what the carrier includes in the quote and whether taxes, duties or handling charges could be collected on delivery.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

What is an OssBuy spreadsheet?

An OssBuy spreadsheet is a shortlist of product links used to compare items before ordering through the shopping agent. Start with a category and verify the original seller listing before choosing a size or color.

Can I order directly from this guide?

The category links open Hipobuyfind for browsing. If you choose to order through OssBuy, copy the original marketplace URL into your OssBuy account and verify that the selected variant matches the seller listing.

What can warehouse QC photos confirm?

Warehouse quality-control photos can show visible features such as the selected color, labels, stitching and obvious damage. Confirm authenticity through verifiable provenance or an authorized seller.

How can I compare OssBuy shipping estimates?

Use your actual destination and the same packed dimensions and weight for each quote. Compare the chargeable weight, tracking, surcharges and any taxes or duties the route includes.
